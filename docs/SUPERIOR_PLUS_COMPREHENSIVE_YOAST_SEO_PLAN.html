Executive summary

The strategy in one page

The site already contains substantial client-approved service information. The priority is to make that information technically unambiguous, easy to crawl, locally credible and connected by a deliberate internal-link structure. Yoast is the control panel; it is not a ranking switch.

No responsible SEO plan can guarantee first place, a specific date, or a Google rich result. This plan improves eligibility, clarity and competitiveness. Rankings remain dependent on competition, location, authority, reviews, links, search demand and Google’s systems.

Layer 1 — Repair

Consolidate duplicate URLs, correct the Service Areas directory, remove thin records from the sitemap, fix schema ownership and confirm every canonical.

Layer 2 — Organise

Give every page one search intent, one primary keyphrase, useful supporting terms and links to its parent, related services and relevant local pages.

Layer 3 — Prove

Add real project evidence, team expertise, verified business details, original images, reviews and local references—not generic suburb-name swapping.

Layer 4 — Publish

Strengthen the 19 approved guides, add author/reviewer and update dates, connect each guide to one service cornerstone, and release consistently.

Layer 5 — Promote

Improve Google Business Profile, consistent business citations, partnerships, project mentions and legitimate local links.

Layer 6 — Measure

Track non-brand clicks, qualified enquiries, calls, quote submissions, indexed pages and local visibility—not only Yoast traffic-light colours.

First five actions

  1. Back up and stage the site; record current traffic, enquiries, indexed pages and rankings.
  2. Choose one canonical URL for every duplicate route family and implement one-hop 301 redirects.
  3. Make one system responsible for title, description, canonical and schema output; verify raw HTML and rendered HTML.
  4. Fix the generic /service-areas/ directory so it is not serving Chadstone-specific metadata/content.
  5. Optimise the core and service pages first; release suburb pages in evidence-backed tiers rather than indexing all 67 at once.

How to handle the older page-to-suburb keyword sheet

The sheet maps terms such as “Painters Mount Waverley” to About and “Interior Painters Glen Waverley” to Interior. Forcing suburb phrases into unrelated Melbourne-wide pages would blur intent and compete with the dedicated suburb pages. This plan keeps:

  • Service pages focused on the service across Melbourne.
  • Suburb pages focused on painters in that suburb, with locally relevant services.
  • About, Process and Contact focused on trust, method and conversion—not used as substitute doorway pages.

Yoast workflow

How to optimise one page safely

1

Confirm the page intent. One page should answer one main need: service, location, company information, process, contact or educational question.

2

Preserve approved copy. Compare the live page with the client PDF. SEO edits may add missing context and links but must not silently change promises or wording.

3

Enter the focus keyphrase. Use it as an editing prompt, not as a ranking guarantee. A red/orange Yoast light is not permission to repeat awkward phrases.

4

Set the SEO title and meta description. Keep them distinct, accurate and compelling. Google may still rewrite either.

5

Set the canonical only when needed. A normal unique page should self-canonicalise. A duplicate should normally be redirected, not merely canonicalised.

6

Select schema type. Choose the page’s actual function. Do not select FAQPage unless the full Q&A is visible.

7

Add internal links. Link upward to a hub, sideways to relevant services/areas, and downward to supporting guides. Use descriptive anchor text.

8

Preview desktop and mobile. Check title wrapping, hero height, long lists, images, floating buttons, forms and accordion accessibility.

9

Publish, purge cache, test. Verify status 200, canonical, indexability, sitemap membership, schema validation and the actual live text.

10

Record the change. Log URL, date, title, meta, focus phrase, links added and baseline Search Console figures.

Aim for useful, natural copy and correct technical output. Do not weaken client-approved language, overstuff suburbs, add filler, or make every indicator green at the expense of readers.

Local SEO architecture

How to handle 67 suburb pages safely

Every suburb page must be more than the same paragraph with a different place name. Google’s spam policies specifically warn against doorway pages and scaled content created mainly to manipulate rankings. The safe approach is staged indexing based on real local usefulness.

TierIndexing ruleMinimum evidence before indexing
Tier 1 Primary client marketsOptimise first and keep indexable once complete.Unique local introduction, property context, suitable services, 2–4 original project images where available, nearby-area links, one testimonial/project reference if genuine, and an accurate CTA.
Tier 2 Adjacent growth marketsIndex in batches after Tier 1 is stable.All Tier 1 quality checks except a local project may be replaced temporarily by detailed suburb-specific property/surface guidance. Add proof before promotion.
Tier 3 Coverage inventoryKeep accessible for users but noindex until genuinely differentiated.Do not index a templated page merely because it exists. Promote only after unique evidence and editorial review.

Per-suburb Yoast pattern

  • Focus keyphrase: painters {Suburb}; secondary phrase: house painters {Suburb}.
  • Title pattern: Painters {Suburb} | House & Commercial Painting. Vary naturally where a verified specialty is stronger.
  • Meta pattern: Professional painting in {Suburb} for homes and commercial properties. Careful preparation, clear quotes and durable finishes. Request a free quote.
  • H1: Professional Painters in {Suburb}. Keep one H1; use H2s for services, local property needs, process, proof, nearby areas and quote CTA.
  • Links: Service Areas hub, 2–4 relevant services, 3–5 genuine neighbouring suburbs, Contact, and one applicable guide.
  • Schema: WebPage + BreadcrumbList through the single approved schema provider. Do not create a fake LocalBusiness branch/address in every suburb.
Avoid: hidden city lists, blocks of repeated suburbs, fake local offices, copied testimonials, invented project claims, or 67 pages published simultaneously with near-identical copy.

Local authority

Signals beyond Yoast

Google Business Profile

  • Exact business name, phone, website and service area.
  • Correct primary and secondary categories.
  • Original project photos added regularly.
  • Services and descriptions aligned with the live site.
  • Respond to reviews without templated keyword stuffing.

Business consistency

  • Use one phone/name format on site, profile and trusted directories.
  • Correct outdated citations; do not create mass low-quality listings.
  • Keep social profiles and business hours current.
  • Only publish a street address if it is genuine and eligible.

Reputation

  • Ask real customers for honest Google reviews.
  • Never offer incentives in exchange for a positive rating.
  • Display approved testimonials accurately.
  • Turn substantial projects into evidence-rich case studies.

Earned links

  • Suppliers, builders, property partners and local organisations.
  • Useful project or maintenance resources worth citing.
  • No purchased link packages, private blog networks or automated guest-post spam.
  • Track referring domains and remove only demonstrably harmful tactics.

Trust details each important page should expose

Who performs/reviews the work; service scope; preparation method; suitable property types; real project imagery; clear quote process; contact details; applicable insurance statement; and a path to About, Gallery, Process and Contact.

Change-control rule

  1. Take a backup and export current Yoast metadata.
  2. Change one URL family or page batch at a time.
  3. Test on staging, then production.
  4. Purge cache and verify the public result.
  5. Record before/after and keep a rollback route.
Release checklist

Per-page acceptance test

Content

  • Client-approved wording is preserved.
  • One visible H1 and logical H2/H3 structure.
  • No invented claims or keyword stuffing.
  • Lists remain readable on mobile.
  • CTA matches the service and leads to Contact.

Yoast

  • Distinct focus keyphrase.
  • Accurate SEO title and meta description.
  • Correct canonical and robots setting.
  • Correct page/article schema type.
  • Social share image/title checked.

Technical

  • Status 200; HTTPS; self-canonical.
  • Only one indexable URL for the content.
  • Included in correct sitemap if indexable.
  • No duplicate title/meta/canonical/schema.
  • Structured data validates.

User experience

  • Desktop and mobile layout tested.
  • Images load; useful alt text; no layout shift.
  • Menu, cards, accordions and links work by touch/keyboard.
  • Floating controls do not obscure copy.
  • Form sends and stores a recoverable enquiry.
